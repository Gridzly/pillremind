How to Use Medminder 💚

Your step-by-step guide to using your Patient Dashboard.

Welcome to Medminder! This guide will help you understand your dashboard, connect with your caregiver, view your medicines, respond to reminders, and review your medication history.

1 Understand your dashboard

Your Patient Dashboard has four main sections:

  • Today: View your scheduled doses for the current day.
  • Medicines: View medicines shared by your caregiver.
  • Reminders: View today's and the next six days' scheduled reminders.
  • History: Review doses you have recorded as taken or missed.

2 Find and share your Patient ID

  1. Find the Patient QR Code panel on your Today dashboard.
  2. Your unique Patient ID appears below the QR code.
  3. Click Copy Patient ID to copy your ID.
  4. You may also show your QR code to your authorized caregiver.
Security tip: Your Patient ID identifies your account. It is not your password. Share it only with someone you trust.

3 Connect with your caregiver

  1. Enable Allow Caregiver Connection Requests.
  2. Wait for your caregiver to send a connection request.
  3. Find the Caregiver Connections panel on your dashboard.
  4. Review the caregiver's name, email, and ID.
  5. Click Approve if you recognize and trust the caregiver.
  6. Click Reject if you do not recognize the request.

Once approved, your caregiver's connection information will appear on your dashboard. Your caregiver can then assign medicines and schedule reminders for you.

Important: Enabling connection requests does not automatically approve anyone. You must approve each request. Your account supports one active caregiver at a time.

4 View your assigned medicines

  1. Open My Medicines.
  2. Review the medicines shared by your caregiver.
  3. Check the medicine name, dosage, strength, and other available details.
  4. Read the instructions provided for each medicine.

Your caregiver manages the medicine information. The patient dashboard is intended for viewing and tracking your assigned medicines.

5 Check today's medicine schedule

  1. Open Today's Schedule.
  2. Review each scheduled medicine.
  3. Check the medicine name, dosage, and scheduled time.
  4. Check the current dose status.

There are three possible dose statuses:

  • Pending: The dose has not yet been recorded.
  • Dose Taken: You have recorded the dose as taken.
  • Dose Missed: You have recorded the dose as missed.

6 Respond to a medicine reminder

When a scheduled reminder becomes due while your dashboard is open, Medminder can display a popup and play a reminder sound.

  • Mark as Taken: Select this after you have actually taken your medicine. The dose will be recorded as taken.
  • Stop Sound: Stops the sound, but keeps the reminder popup open.
Stopping the sound does not mean the medicine has been taken. Make sure you record the correct dose status.

7 View your upcoming reminders

  1. Open My Reminders.
  2. Review today's reminders and those scheduled for the following six days.
  3. Check the date, medicine name, dosage, time, and status.
  4. Use the available actions to record the dose as taken or missed.

8 Record a dose manually

  1. Open Today's Schedule or My Reminders.
  2. Find the correct scheduled medicine.
  3. Select Mark as Taken if you took the dose.
  4. Select Mark as Missed if you missed the dose.
  5. Wait for the confirmation message.

Successfully recorded doses will be reflected in your schedule and intake history.

9 Review your intake history

  1. Open Intake History.
  2. Review your previously recorded doses.
  3. Check the medicine name, dosage, date, recorded time, and status.

Your history displays records marked as Taken or Missed.

10 Disconnect your caregiver

  1. Find the Caregiver Connections section.
  2. Review your active caregiver information.
  3. Select Disconnect Caregiver.
  4. Confirm the action if you want to proceed.

After disconnecting, shared medicines and reminders will no longer appear on your dashboard. Your existing intake history will remain in your account.

11 Sign out safely

Click Sign Out when you finish using Medminder. Always sign out when using a shared or public computer.

Important reminders 💚

  • Keep your account password private.
  • Approve connection requests only from caregivers you trust.
  • Check your medicine details and instructions regularly.
  • Record your doses accurately.
  • Keep your Patient Dashboard open and connected to the internet to receive browser-based reminder popups and sounds. These reminders are not guaranteed when the browser is closed or your device is turned off.
  • If medicine information looks incorrect, contact your caregiver or healthcare professional before taking the medicine.
  • Medminder helps organize medication schedules and does not replace professional medical advice.

Thank you for using Medminder!
Stay organized and take care of yourself.
